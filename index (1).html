Our Story

A Sanctuary of
Refined Comfort

Gold Dragon Hotel stands as Mardan's premier destination for travelers seeking quality accommodation with genuine hospitality. Nestled in Erum Colony, our property combines modern comforts with the warmth of traditional Pashtun welcome.

From solo travelers to families, our guests consistently praise the cleanliness, attentive staff, and the peaceful environment that makes every stay truly memorable. We are currently expanding our on-site restaurant to further elevate your dining experience.

Open 24 hours, 365 days — because your comfort never takes a day off.
